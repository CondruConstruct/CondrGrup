Information

Privacy
policy.

Condr Grup S.R.L. uses only the data necessary to respond to requests received through the site.

Data collected

Forms may collect name, contact details, city, job type and information voluntarily entered by the user.

Purpose of processing

Data is used to respond to requests, discuss projects, check reviews and, only with consent, publish a review.

Submission of forms

The site is hosted through GitHub Pages, and the forms use the external service FormSubmit to send the message to the Condr Grup address. We do not ask for passwords, bank details or other sensitive information through the form.

Retention and deletion

We keep the messages as long as necessary to analyze the request and continue the collaboration. You can request access, correction or deletion of the data sent by e-mail.

Reviews

Reviews are not published automatically. Condr Grup checks them and asks for publication consent. The user can ask to display the full name, first name, initials or not to publish.

Contact

For questions or data requests: condru01@gmail.com.
