What matters in a similar project

In a commercial objective, demolition must be coordinated with access, evacuation of materials, protection of preserved areas and the schedule of other teams. For a fair offer, we analyze the situation in the field and clearly define the work package.

  • establishing access and evacuation;
  • delimitation of responsibilities;
  • scheduling execution steps.
